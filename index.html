About me.

I'm an investment professional with a background in computer science engineering, currently working in the Founder's Office at a99 VC, focused on manufacturing and infrastructure.

My work involves studying companies, assessing technical differentiation, mapping markets, comparing competing solutions and translating complexity into clear investment perspectives.

Earlier, I was a working partner at Cappricio Securities, a cybersecurity services business. That experience introduced me to client requirements, service delivery and the commercial realities behind technical work.

Investment thinking

An analytical framework
I continue to refine.

01 /

Define the customer problem

Identify the physical, operational, or economic bottleneck and the cost of the current alternative.

02 /

Validate technical differentiation

Examine architecture, benchmarks, qualification requirements, and competing solutions.

03 /

Model market and adoption economics

Study customer segments, purchase cycles, unit economics, market structure, and scale constraints.

04 /

Test defensibility

Distinguish durable intellectual property, specialized production expertise and switching costs from features that competitors can easily reproduce.

05 /

Communicate uncertainty and judgment

Distinguish verified facts from assumptions and isolate the questions that should determine the investment decision.
